notes

we're hack united, a nonprofit run by high schoolers.

7 hackathons in, 5,000+ builders from 50+ countries have shown up to learn, ship, and demo with us, and $30,000+ in prizes have already gone home with them.

we believe the code is only half of it. teamwork, communication, and presenting your work are part of the craft.

united hacks v8 is three days to build something you're proud of, with workshops, mentors, and $25k+ in prizes along the way.

it's free. it's online. bring your friends.|
